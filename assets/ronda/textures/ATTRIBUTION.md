# Ronda surface scans

All textures are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) under the [CC0 1.0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license) (public domain). Credit is not required but is given here with thanks. `tools/prepare-ronda-textures.mjs` resized each 1K colour (grayscale detail for the cliff and bridge stone), normal (OpenGL) and AO/roughness/metal map to 1024 and 512 px and encoded it as KTX2 (ETC1S).

- `rock_face_03` (cliff): [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03) by Dario Barresi, Rico Cilliers
- `large_sandstone_blocks_01` (sandstone): [Large Sandstone Blocks 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/large_sandstone_blocks_01) by Rob Tuytel
- `clay_roof_tiles_02` (roof): [Clay Roof Tiles 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/clay_roof_tiles_02) by Amal Kumar
- `cobblestone_floor_04` (cobble): [Cobblestone Floor 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_04) by Rob Tuytel
- `plastered_stone_wall` (whitewash): [Plastered Stone Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_stone_wall) by Rob Tuytel
