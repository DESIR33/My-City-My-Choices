# City surface scans

All textures are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) under the [CC0 1.0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license) (public domain). Credit is not required but is given here with thanks. `tools/prepare-city-textures.mjs` converted each color scan to a grayscale tone map (so buildings keep their own paint colors), resized to 1024 and 512 pixels and compressed to KTX2 (Basis ETC1S). White Planks Clean is turned a quarter turn to read as horizontal lap siding. Normal and roughness maps for scans the Maple House already uses live in `assets/house/textures/`.
